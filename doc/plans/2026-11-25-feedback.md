## Design

- Source of truth for "what happened": `store.onFeedback` (`ticks` with every tick's events of one `advance`, `command` with the events a command added, `reset` on a replaced state). Loaded states never replay events.
- Sound (`src/ui/sound/`): pure `cues.ts` (events to cues, repeat gaps, at most 3 voices), thin `device.ts` (Web Audio, context only after a user gesture while on), `controller.ts` (preference under `kitchen-line.sound.v1`, silent while the page is hidden).
- Cues: sale, customer lost (not closing-time leftovers), spoiled or binned (at most one per 1.5 s), day end, goal or end screen, purchase (tier, kitchen size, upgrade, branch open or refit). Placing buildings is silent.
