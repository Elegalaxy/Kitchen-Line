# Dish pack (restaurant tier)

Status: approved roadmap item R3 (2026-11-24). The restaurant menu cap stays 6 (user decision).

Goal: two new restaurant-tier dishes on buildings players already know. Fish and chips (new fresh ingredient fish -> fryer -> assembler with fries, so it competes with fries for the fryer) and Cheeseburger (new ingredient cheese, an assembler variant of the burger). Both are bought in the Upgrades tab with the existing recipe-unlock command. New `DishDef.requiresTier`: a dish of a higher tier is not in the customers' taste pool at lower tiers. Stall tier: hashes and fixtures unchanged. Restaurant hashes may change deliberately (recorded below).

Rules: one commit per step after `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npx vitest run --exclude src/harness/perf.test.ts`. Allowed status values: `todo`, `done`, `verified`, `failed`, `blocked`.

Working folder: `D:\Coding\project\Kitchen-Line-worktrees\dish-pack`.

Design decisions (provisional numbers, all in content):
- Fish: raw, pantry cost 150, spoils like fresh produce (600 ticks). Fryer recipe fish -> friedFish (60 ticks, same as fries). Fish and chips = assembler of friedFish + fries. Ref price 900, popularity 2, unlock $120. Ingredient cost 180 (fish 150 + potato 30), margin 720 (5.0x), like salad/sandwich/rice bowl.
- Cheese: raw, pantry cost 60, no spoil (like patty). Cheeseburger = assembler of bun + cookedPatty + cheese (3 inputs; the tomato of the plain burger is replaced). Ref price 1000, popularity 3 (like the burger; at 2 a cheeseburger-only line earned less than the plain burger line in the harness), unlock $150. Cost 230, margin 770 (4.3x; burger is 3.8x).
- Taste pool: dishes whose `requiresTier` (default stall) is at or below the current tier; a dish with an explicit `requiresTier` must also be unlocked. Dishes without it keep today's rule (in the pool even when locked), so stall draws are identical.
- `unlockRecipe` of a higher-tier dish is rejected with 'locked by tier'; the Menu and Upgrades tabs hide such dishes at lower tiers.

### Step 4: Harness and measurements
- Status: `done`
- Layouts added (cheap, 8 x 6 grid, dev loader sets the restaurant tier through the new `LayoutSetup.tier`): `fishAndChipsLine` ($198 build) and `cheeseburgerLine` ($172). Both are covered by the `layouts.test.ts` "serves at least 5 in 1200 ticks" loop. The scripted journey player is NOT taught the new dishes (it never buys them), so the journey is unchanged by design.
- `npm run sim -- --journey --seeds 20` (on origin/main with the pro-fryer player): 20/20 reach the end screen, 0 loss days in 374, end-screen median day 19 (min 17, max 20; the earlier run before the pack and before the pro-fryer player was 0 of 383, median 19, max 21 - the difference is the pro-stations player change, not this pack). Median 13 days from the upgrade to the end screen. The pack cannot change the journey: it never unlocks the new dishes, so the tiered taste pool is the same (restaurant journey hashes are unchanged by this pack).
- The 9 layouts `--layout X --seed demo --days 5`: byte-identical to before (checked with `cmp` on the printed output, including stateHash).
- New dishes alone, `--layout fishAndChipsLine|cheeseburgerLine --seed demo --days 5` (restaurant tier, 8 x 6): 5-day profit $697.80 and $880.50 (fish and chips served 33/31/15/15/17, cheeseburger 33/29/22/22/21 a day, popularity 3; never a loss day).
- Fair comparison at the restaurant tier (all single-dish lines, tier set to restaurant, 5 seeds x 5 days, pantries paused for the last 400 ticks): profit/day fries $101.5, soup $70.6, salad $151.9, sandwich $119.7, rice bowl $115.4, burger $128.1, cheeseburger $142.5 (popularity 3; was $98.5 at popularity 2), fish and chips $117.2. Margin per sale (after wasted ingredients) burger $5.14, cheeseburger $6.47, fish and chips $5.95: inside the mid-price range.
- Known limits: the fryer shared by fries and fish is 2 fryer-passes per fish and chips dish (fish and fries each take 60 ticks), so a combined fries + fish and chips line needs the pro fryer or a second fryer; not simulated (no layout, the player does not build it).
